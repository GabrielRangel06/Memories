# Fati — a thread becomes a time capsule

## Director’s treatment

- **Promise:** 61 ordinary screenshots can make one friendship feel like a place you can return to.
- **Identity:** After-hours archive editorial crossed with a precision motion-graphics UI: ink-plum, warm archive paper, a persimmon signal red, Instrument Serif display against DM Sans and Space Mono labels.
- **Hero:** The original Discord conversation screenshots, treated as real artifacts rather than recreated UI.
- **Motion:** A compound 2.5D camera grammar—shallow Y-axis panel turns, X-axis hinges, restrained Z-spins, split-card flips, orbiting camera offsets, and brief whip-pans—gives each onset-cut screenshot a distinct entrance and exit. A persistent vermilion archive rail, corner registration marks, and parallax grid carry continuity without repainting or materially distorting source content.
- **Arc:** Start in the football banter at full speed, move through a broader run of shared jokes, then let the last intimate fragments breathe.
- **Payoff:** Screenshot 60 contracts into a memory field; screenshot 61 resolves full-frame as the final artifact. The top archive rule and restrained camera position match the opening for a clean loop.
- **Format:** 16:9 landscape.

### Revised whole-film choreography — shot-by-shot, before implementation

The canonical timing and source assignment above are locked. The 61 original screenshots are each mounted once, unaltered, as a single `object-fit: contain` image. The image plane itself never gets perspective-distorted beyond a legible shallow angle (maximum 9°); the virtual camera, paper-dark surround, duplicate soft-focus underlay, registration marks, and archive rail create depth around it. The content remains the visual hero. Each cut is compound: a distinct 3D panel action + a mask or scale change + the same persimmon rail crossing the seam. Directions alternate to prevent a repetitive carousel. Long holds get a second camera/action beat and begin their outgoing movement in the final fifth.

**Common shot clock:** At each listed onset, the incoming full screenshot is already visible as a contained plate. Over 0.00–0.32s a changing shutter/aperture plus shallow 3D orientation resolves it; 0.12–0.75s the camera catches its named pose; near the middle, the named secondary action shifts focus without obscuring text; in the final 18–22% the exit action begins, overlapping the incoming plate. Durations under 0.45s use a single 0.12–0.18s compound punctuation, then their remaining time is a readable settle / outgoing preparation. Every per-shot image hold has its own deterministic camera drift; no decorative transition delays or timing extensions are added.
